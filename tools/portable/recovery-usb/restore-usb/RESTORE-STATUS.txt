HOST-ONLY RESTORE ARCHIVE

This archive has host fixture coverage only. Target-vehicle NEMESIS emergency
dispatch, toolbox mount behavior, SELinux label tools, and exact restore have
not been verified. Do not use this archive in the vehicle.

The restore script requires a validated manifest and immutable originals at
/data/local/gen5w-portable/persistent/recovery/. The persistent installer
that creates those files is not implemented or enabled in this build.
